WHO WE ARE

Building human capabilities in an AI-enabled world.

The AI for Competence Development Lab explores how AI can meaningfully support the knowledge, skills, attitudes and dispositions people need to learn, work and thrive.

We bring together educational research, learning sciences and emerging technologies to develop evidence-informed practices and solutions for learners and educators.
